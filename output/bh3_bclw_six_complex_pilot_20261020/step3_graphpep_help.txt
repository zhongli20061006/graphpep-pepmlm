usage: step3_graphpep.py [-h] [--lists LISTS] [--pdb-dir PDB_DIR]
                         [--scores SCORES] [--graphpep-dir GRAPHPEP_DIR]
                         [--patched-resmap PATCHED_RESMAP]
                         [--blue-threshold BLUE_THRESHOLD]

Score each real complex with its own receptor. Raw GraphPep score: lower
better. Different sequences cannot share a multi-MODEL topology or ESM
feature. Optional manifest columns: complex_path, model_id, receptor_sequence.
Confidence columns are preserved. Empty interaction graphs stay unscored.

options:
  -h, --help            show this help message and exit
  --lists LISTS
  --pdb-dir PDB_DIR
  --scores SCORES
  --graphpep-dir GRAPHPEP_DIR
  --patched-resmap PATCHED_RESMAP
  --blue-threshold BLUE_THRESHOLD
